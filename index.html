LENA Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

LENA ("LENA", "we", "us", or "our") provides a mobile application used by property appraisers and inspectors to capture, organize, and report on-site inspection data ("the App"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Before publishing: replace the contact email in the "Contact us" section below with your real support address, and add your legal entity name if you have one.

Information we collect

The App collects the following categories of information. All of it is tied to your account or the inspection records you create — none of it is used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

CategoryExamples
IdentifiersName, email address, phone number, account/user ID
AddressProperty/job site physical address
Photos & videosInspection and damage photos you capture or import
Audio dataVoice notes you record during an inspection
Other user contentNotes, inspection forms, damage assessments, sketches, and PDF reports you create
Financial infoCost and estimate figures you enter as part of an appraisal (not payment-card data)

How we use this information

  • To operate core app functionality: creating and syncing jobs, photos, voice notes, forms, and reports across your devices.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your session secure.
  • To let members of your team access shared job records where you've enabled that access.
  • To generate the PDF inspection reports the App produces on your behalf.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Where your data is stored

Inspection data is cached locally on your device and synced to our backend, hosted on Supabase, which provides our database, authentication, and file storage. Supabase acts as our service provider and processes data only on our behalf. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Your sign-in session is stored on-device using the operating system's secure credential storage, optionally protected by Face ID/Touch ID.

Data retention and account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time from Settings > Delete Account in the App. Once submitted, we will delete your account and eligible personal data. Appraisal records owned by your company/team may be retained where required by law or by the organization's own record-keeping obligations, even after your personal account is deleted.

Your choices

  • Camera, microphone, photo library, and speech-recognition access are all optional and requested only when you use the related feature; you can change permissions any time in your device's Settings app.
  • You can review, edit, or delete inspection content you've created directly in the App.
  • You can request account and data deletion as described above.

Children's privacy

LENA is a professional tool for appraisers and inspectors and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
